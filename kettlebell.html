Kettlebell Circuit

Full Body, 5 Days

A Mon–Fri morning program built around one 25lb bell and a 7′ basement ceiling. Strength and conditioning days alternate so the same muscles aren't hammered five days straight, and every overhead move is done seated to stay well clear of the rafters.

20–23 min Full body Ceiling-safe 25lb bell
Before you start: a small carb-forward bite 15–20 min out (banana, dates, toast with honey) keeps energy up without sitting heavy this early. All pressing work is done seated — that's what keeps a full overhead lockout under your 7′ ceiling. Standing swings and pulls stay capped at chest height on purpose; don't add push press or snatches into this mix.
